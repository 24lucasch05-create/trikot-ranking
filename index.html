  1. Visit the kit page on FootballKitArchive
  2. Right-click the kit image → "Save image as..."
  3. Save it to the correct folder with the right filename

Example: images/bundesliga/bayern-munchen-home.png

Or click on any jersey card to open its FootballKitArchive page.
